## Research Goals

This research aims to understand the differences between clinic-based scheduling at VistA (Veterans Health Information Systems and Technology Architecture) facilities and provider-based scheduling at Oracle Health facilities within the VA healthcare system. The findings will inform design decisions to improve the Veteran's healthcare experience and guide the backend team's approach to provider-based and multi-modality scheduling.

---

## Methodology

We used contextual inquiry and semi-structured interviews with MSA (Medical Support Assistant) scheduling staff at one Oracle Health and four VistA facilities within the VA healthcare system.

---

## Hypotheses and Conclusions

We found that the effectiveness of scheduling systems—whether provider-based, clinic-based, or resource-based—is significantly influenced by how they are designed, implemented, and operated. These conclusions are based on comparing Oracle Health's resource-based and VistA's clinic-based systems by observing how each is utilized by clinic and scheduling staff.
